Additional Details:

Purpose: Establish premium flights to larger international airports throughout the world.

Main configurations consist of three classes: Economy, Business, and First Class.

Great for high density flights from hubs worldwide. Possess the ability for in-flight caterin and boost profit returns.

Advantages: The aircraft is capable fo handling 350 passengers in a single class configuration. It has great usage of fuel at 19 lbs per km considering the aircraft's large frame and large passenger seating. The aircraft uses the GE90 engines, which are one of the most powerful engines in the world. It has a maximum cruising speed of 1,036 kph. It is capable of traveling up to a maximum distance of 14,658 km in a single trip to any airport qualified to accommodate it. The aircraft has low maintenance with an A-check every 460 hours.

Disadvantages: The aircraft has such a large frame and heavy takeoff weight for dual-engines that it requires a long runway requirement of 11,000 ft. The aircraft requires more runway than the B747-800 and the A380-800, which greatly limits its ability to land at certain airports. The aircraft cost is ~$68,000,000 because of its large frame and the GE90 engines. The cost of A-Checks is ~$5,800,000. Slow turnaround times.
